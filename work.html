Presentation case study

Entering Mexico, one region at a time

Client
Norvale Foods × Ashcombe Lane Partners
Industry
Consumer goods · Market entry
Deck
Board discussion document
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

The board wanted a nationwide launch. The analysis said otherwise: premium demand sits in a few metros, and going alone is slow and capital-heavy. The deck had to change the board's mind without a fight.

Approach

I built the story around one decision. Three ideas up front, then a four-phase roadmap (Prove, Expand, Scale, Lead), a regional comparison table and a single-photo pause before the ask. Cormorant Garamond and Inter on cream, black and olive give it the calm authority of a strategy memo.

Outcome

The board sees the recommendation on page one and the evidence in the order they'd challenge it. Every slide points to one approval: a phased entry with a distribution partner. Figures such as 61% of premium demand in three metros are part of the deck's storyline.

Presentation case study

Annual Report 2025

Client
Fenwyck Trust
Industry
Philanthropy · Libraries
Deck
Annual report for donors
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

A library foundation had its strongest year and needed donors and partners to feel it in seconds. The usual charity-brochure tone would have undersold the work.

Approach

I set a strict Swiss system: Inter Tight only, black, white and one muted red, on a visible six-column grid. Big numbers lead, followed by a ten-year giving chart, one library story told through a librarian's voice, and a use-of-funds page that ends with next year's goals.

Outcome

Donors get the headline in under a minute and the detail on a second read. The grid keeps every page scannable and makes the report easy to extend year after year. Figures such as 412 libraries and $12.4M granted belong to the report's narrative.

Presentation case study

Brand proposal

Client
Casa Ascua
Industry
Hospitality · Boutique hotel
Deck
Branding proposal
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

A 12-room 19th-century house in Jalatlaco, Oaxaca, was reopening under a forgettable name, competing on price and dependent on booking platforms. The owners needed a reason to invest in a brand, not just a logo.

Approach

I wrote the proposal like a story first and a quote second. It opens on the idea behind the name ("here the ember never goes out: it is shared, cared for and inherited"), then sets the problem in numbers, four guest moments and a clear scope, timeline and investment. Tenor Sans and Manrope on near-black green, clay and cream set the mood before a word is read.

Outcome

The owners can approve scope and budget in one meeting: five deliverables over 16 weeks, with the brand idea doing the selling. Occupancy and platform figures come from the proposal's brief.

Presentation case study

The Dune Collection

Client
Ardel Ceramics
Industry
Craft · Home goods
Deck
Pre-sale deck for retailers
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

A hand-thrown stoneware studio in Porto was pitching its first collection to multi-brand stores. It needed to win shelf space without losing its handmade character or competing on price.

Approach

I let restraint carry the message. One bold line ("Made slower."), large fields of deep blue, stone and sand in a light geometric sans (Outfit), the six pieces with glazes and retail prices, a year-one forecast by channel and dated next steps.

Outcome

Buyers see the product, the price and the deadline without hunting for them, and the deck closes on a single ask: confirm opening orders. Pre-order and forecast figures are part of the pitch's storyline.

Presentation case study

Annual program 2027

Client
Museo Cerro Alto
Industry
Culture · Museum
Deck
Board proposal
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

Audience growth had stalled at a Monterrey art museum, and the board asked for a program that would bring in people who had never visited.

Approach

I framed the year as an invitation, not a calendar. A full-bleed photo statement ("A museum isn't visited: it's inhabited") opens, followed by four exhibitions across the year, an audience chart and a budget split with key dates. Newsreader serif with italics, IBM Plex Mono labels, sand and ink, and no accent color: the art provides the color.

Outcome

The board can vote on program and budget in one sitting, with free Sundays as the headline move. Visitor and budget targets are part of the proposal's narrative.
