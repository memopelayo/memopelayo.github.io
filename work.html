Presentation case study

Entering Mexico, one region at a time

Client
Norvale Foods × Ashcombe Lane Partners
Industry
Consumer goods · Market entry
Deck
Board discussion document
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

A premium pantry brand's board wanted to launch across all of Mexico at once. The analysis behind the deck pointed the other way: premium demand is concentrated, and going it alone would be slow and capital-heavy.

Approach

A classic editorial deck — Cormorant Garamond and Inter on cream, black and olive — that leads with three ideas, then walks the board through a four-phase roadmap (Prove, Expand, Scale, Lead), a single-photo divider and a regional comparison table.

Outcome

The story is built to one decision: approve a phased entry with a distribution partner. In the deck's narrative, three metros hold 61% of the premium market and a partner cuts time-to-shelf from 22 to 9 months.

Presentation case study

Annual Report 2025

Client
Fenwyck Trust
Industry
Philanthropy · Libraries
Deck
Annual report for donors
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

A foundation that funds community libraries had its best year yet and needed a report for donors and partners that felt clear and confident — not like a charity brochure.

Approach

A Swiss/modern system: Inter Tight only, white, black and a muted red, with a visible six-column grid. Big numbers first, a ten-year giving chart, one library story with a librarian's quote, and a use-of-funds breakdown with next year's goals.

Outcome

A report that reads in a minute and holds up on a second look. Within the deck's story: 412 libraries in 9 countries, 1.8M books in circulation and $12.4M granted in 2025.

Presentation case study

Brand proposal

Client
Casa Ascua
Industry
Hospitality · Boutique hotel
Deck
Branding proposal
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

A 12-room 19th-century house in Jalatlaco, Oaxaca, reopening in April 2027 under a forgettable name, competing on price and depending on booking platforms.

Approach

A dark, contemporary deck — Tenor Sans and Manrope on near-black green, clay and cream — that moves from concept ("here the ember never goes out: it is shared, cared for and inherited") to the challenge in numbers, four guest moments, and a clear scope, timeline and investment.

Outcome

Designed to get owners to approve scope and budget in one meeting: five deliverables over 16 weeks, with the brand story doing the selling. Figures (68% occupancy, 74% via platforms) are part of the brief.

Presentation case study

The Dune Collection

Client
Ardel Ceramics
Industry
Craft · Home goods
Deck
Pre-sale deck for retailers
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

A hand-thrown stoneware studio in Porto launching its first collection to multi-brand stores — without losing its handmade character or competing on price.

Approach

A light geometric sans (Outfit) over large fields of deep blue, stone and sand. One bold statement ("Made slower."), the six pieces with retail prices and glazes, a year-one forecast by channel, and dated next steps.

Outcome

A deck that ends with a clear ask: confirm opening orders before the deadline. In the deck's story, 2,600 pre-orders and letters of intent from 14 stores support a 12,000-piece first year.

Presentation case study

Annual programme 2027

Client
Museo Cerro Alto
Industry
Culture · Museum
Deck
Board proposal
Role
Presentation design

Challenge

An art museum in Monterrey whose audience growth slowed; the board asked for a plan to reach people who have never visited.

Approach

A light editorial deck — Newsreader serif with italics and IBM Plex Mono labels, sand and ink with no accent colour. A full-bleed photo statement ("A museum isn't visited: it's inhabited"), four exhibitions across the year, an audience chart and a budget split with key dates.

Outcome

Built for a board vote on programme and budget. Within the deck's story, the goal is 110k visitors in 2027 (+17%) on an MXN 18.5M budget, with free Sundays as the headline move.
